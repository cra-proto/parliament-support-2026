Using Canada Post Connect

To create a Canada Post Connect participant account, follow the instructions on the Participant PDF on the Connect (Canada Post) website.

The Problem Resolution service at the CRA has created some frequently asked questions and a demonstration video for MP constituency offices.

The frequently asked questions covers:

  • Common questions about roles and access in Connect
  • Intake vs. private conversations
  • Staff changes, and more

Refer to: Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ) (Doc, 230 KB)

The demonstration video covers:

  • How to access your Connect account
  • How to access your office’s intake conversation
  • How to securely submit a PR referral

Watch the demonstration video or view the transcript.

Technical support for Canada Post Connect

For all Connect related technical questions, call Canada Post Connect support at 1-877-376-1212 (opens up phone application)1-877-376-1212.

Contact Connect support for:

  • Login issues or password resets
  • Account verification
  • System errors
  • Session timeouts
  • Other technical issues related to the Connect platform itself

If the issue is not resolved through Canada Post Connect support, contact Problem Resolution HQ. 

Help with the Problem Resolution service

The Problem Resolution service service does not provide technical support for Connect accounts, but will assist with Problem Resolution service-specific intake and process questions as required.

Contact Problem Resolution HQ for other questions about:

  • Submitting referrals
  • Intake processes
  • Changes to participants in your office’s intake conversation

Problem Resolution service team leaders

For urgent situations or follow-up after the 5 business day acknowledgment period, contact the appropriate Problem Resolution service team leader using the contact information provided to MP offices.
