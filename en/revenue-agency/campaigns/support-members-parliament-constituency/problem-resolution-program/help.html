Technical help for Canada Post Connect

For all Connect related technical questions, call Canada Post Connect support (1-877-376-1212)

Contact Connect support for:

  • login issues or password resets
  • account verification;
  • system errors;
  • session timeouts; or
  • other technical issues related to the Connect platform itself.

If the issue is not resolved through Canada Post Connect support, contact Problem Resolution HQ. 

Help with the Problem Resolution service

The Problem Resolution service service does not provide technical support for Canada Post Connect accounts, but will assist with Problem Resolution service-specific intake and process questions as required.

Contact Problem Resolution HQ for other questions about:

  • submitting referrals;
  • intake processes; or
  • changes to participants in your office’s intake conversation.

Problem Resolution service Team Leaders

For urgent situations or follow-up after the five business day acknowledgment period, contact the appropriate Problem Resolution service Team Leader using the contact information provided to MP offices.
