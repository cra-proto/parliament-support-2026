After you make a submission

Confirmation of Receipt

Once your message appears in your office’s Connect intake conversation, it has been received by the PR service. No separate email confirmation is sent.

The intake conversation itself serves as the official record of submission. If you can see the message in the conversation, PR can see it as well. If you do not see the message, PR has not received it.

In accordance with PR service standards, an officer will contact the constituency assistant who submitted the request within five (5) business days. There is no need to follow up before this period has passed.

Acknowledgment from an officer

A Problem Resolution officer will contact the constituency assistant who submitted the request within five (5) business days, in accordance with PR service standards.

Service Standards and Follow-Up

Once a referral is submitted through your office’s intake conversation and appears in the conversation, it has been received by PR.

In accordance with PR service standards:

  • A PR officer will contact the constituency assistant who submitted the request within five (5) business days.
  • Most issues are resolved within fifteen (15) business days.
    • If additional time is required, PR will provide an update and, when possible, an anticipated completion date.

Following up after submission

Please do not follow up before the five-business-day acknowledgement period has passed.

If no officer has contacted your office after five-business-days, contact the appropriate PR Team Leader using the contact information provided to MP offices.
