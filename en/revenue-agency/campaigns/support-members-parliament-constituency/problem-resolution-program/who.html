Who the service is for

The Problem Resolution Program is available to Members of Parliament and their constituency offices

Authorized staff may contact the CRA through PRP on behalf of people represented by their parliamentarian.

Reasons to use PRP

Special intervention may be required there is a:

  • situation that causes financial hardship, for example, when there is a delay in processing a payment, and the taxpayer depends on it to cover essential needs such as housing, food, medical expenses, and utilities;
  • situation that requires a high degree of sensitivity, for example, where a taxpayer experiences personal difficulties such as a serious illness or a personal or family problem;
  • recurring problem, or part of a trend, resulting in many requests for the same type of matter; or
  • situation where an Agency error was identified.
