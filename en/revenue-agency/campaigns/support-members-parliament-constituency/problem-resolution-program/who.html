Who the service is for

The Problem Resolution Program is available to Members of Parliament and their constituency offices

Authorized staff may contact the CRA through PRP on behalf of people represented by their parliamentarian.

Authorized Constituency Office Staff

Members of Parliament may experience staff turnover in their constituency offices. To ensure the security of taxpayer information, it is essential that the Authorized Constituency Office Staff Form is kept current and accurately reflects the authorized personnel for each MP office.

Whenever there are changes to staff – whether someone joins or leaves the office – MPs must submit an updated form. Please send the updated form through your office’s Connect intake conversation and confirm which staff member requires access to submit referrals via Connect. 

Keeping this list updated helps us:

  • Identify and validate constituency assistants authorized to act on behalf of their respective MP.
  • Prevent unauthorized individuals from accessing taxpayer information.

We ask MPs to regularly review and update the Authorized Constituency Office Staff Form (PDF, 153 KB) to ensure accurate records are maintained.
