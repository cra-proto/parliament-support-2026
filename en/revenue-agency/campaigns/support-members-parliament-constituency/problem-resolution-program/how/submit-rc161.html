Confidentiality

The confidentiality provisions of the various acts administered by the Canada Revenue Agency (CRA) prevent the disclosure of taxpayer information to unauthorized individuals. For us to release taxpayer information to your office, ensure that:

  • You submit Form RC161, Authorization for Parliamentarians, fully completed, dated and signed by the constituent.  
    • Authorization validity: Form RC161 is valid for six (6) months or until the authorized issue is resolved, whichever comes first. A new form is required if a different issue arises. 
  • You identify yourself and your constituency office.
  • Provide all necessary supporting documents related to the issue. For example:
    • For address updates, include a completed RC325 form.
    • For CCB applications, provide a completed RC66 form.
    • For a DTC application, submit a completed T2201 form.

If your enquiry is for general information and does not require access to specific taxpayer information, then Form RC161 is not required.

Submitting Form RC161 with electronic signature

Problem Resolution accepts electronic signatures from taxpayers on the PDF version of the RC161 found on Canada.ca.

To ensure the fillable PDF form is used correctly and all features – including electronic signatures – are accessible, it is important to follow these steps:

  1. Download the fillable RC161 PDF to your computer.
  2. Open the downloaded PDF using Acrobat Reader 10 or later.
  3. Avoid opening the PDF directly in your web browser, as this may limit functionality.

When a taxpayer submits an electronically signed form RC161 to their Member of Parliament, it is the constituency office’s responsibility to validate the taxpayer’s identity before sending it to Problem Resolution. The taxpayer’s identity can be validated by obtaining a photo ID (i.e. a valid driver’s license or a health card).
