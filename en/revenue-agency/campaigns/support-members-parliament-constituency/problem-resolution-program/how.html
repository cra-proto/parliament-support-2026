Before you submit

Review the checklist below to make sure that your enquiry is ready to be actioned by the CRA:

  • All steps were taken to ensure confidentiality
  • The constituent was unable use the previously detailed publically available channels to find a resolution
  • The information requested was not available on the MP constituency office web page
  • All necessary supporting documents related to the issue are provided, for example:
    • For address updates, include a completed Form RC325
    • For CCB applications, provide a completed Form RC66
    • For a DTC application, submit a completed Form T2201

Failing to verify each step outlined in the checklist may impact the ability to meet service standards.

Confidentiality

The confidentiality provisions of the various acts administered by the CRA prevent the disclosure of taxpayer information to unauthorized individuals.

To release taxpayer information to your office, ensure that:

  • You validate the taxpayer’s identity
  • You identify yourself and your constituency office
  • A completed Form RC161 is submitted, unless your enquiry is for general information and does not require access to specific taxpayer information

Submitting a request

All Problem Resolution referrals must be submitted through Connect. Connect is a secure service approved for the transmission of protected information. Fax and email are no longer accepted.

A Connect conversation has been created for each MP office which is where you submit your referrals. If you did not receive an invitation email for your intake conversation from Connect, send an email to Problem Resolution HQ.

To find resources for Connect, refer to Getting help with the service.

Connect (Canada Post)

Problem Resolution has onboarded to Connect as the intake method for Members of Parliament referrals, and for securely exchanging confidential messages and documents with MP offices.

The Connect platform itself is secure, so documents or protected information do not need to be redacted.

Connect provides:

  • Improved security: Eliminate risks associated with fax and unsecured email
  • Better workflow: Streamlines the management of referrals and document exchange
  • Enhanced traceability: Provides a clear audit trail for all communication
  • Modernization supports: Enables the PR service to move away from legacy tools and adopt secure digital processes
