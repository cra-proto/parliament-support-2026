Before you submit

Please review the checklist below to make sure that your enquiry is ready to be actioned by the CRA:

  • You verified the constituent’s identity. The constituent attempted to resolve the issue through normal CRA channels.
  • The constituent signed and dated the RC161, Authorization for Parliamentarians.
  • The issue and reason for priority are clearly described on Form RC161. Indicated on the RC161 the reason why the constituent chose to come through a Member of Parliament’s (MP) office instead of going through the normal channels (for example, has tried the normal channels, has trouble navigating the CRA webpage, has a language barrier).
  • The information requested was not available on the MP constituency office web page.
  • All supporting documentation are attached.

Although we strive to resolve all cases promptly, failing to verify each step outlined in the checklist may impact our ability to meet service standards.

How to submit your request to us

All Problem Resolution  referrals must be submitted through Canada Post Connect (Connect).  Connect is a secure service approved for the transmission of protected information. Fax and email are no longer accepted.

A Connect conversation has been created for each MP office which is where you submit your referrals to us.  If you did not receive an invitation email for your intake conversation from Connect, please send an email to: Problem Resolution HQ.

Canada Post Connect (Connect)

Problem Resolution has onboarded to Canada Post Connect (Connect) as the new intake method for Members of Parliament (MP) referrals, and for securely exchanging confidential messages and documents with MP offices, and because the platform itself is secure, documents or protected information do not need to be redacted.

Connect is a secure digital platform that allows the exchange of confidential information while ensuring privacy, traceability, and compliance with CRA security standards.

  • Improved security: Eliminate risks associated with fax and unsecured email.
  • Better workflow: Streamlines the management of referrals and document exchange.
  • Enhanced traceability: Provides a clear audit trail for all communication.
  • Supports modernization: Enables the PR service to move away from legacy tools and adopt secure digital processes.
