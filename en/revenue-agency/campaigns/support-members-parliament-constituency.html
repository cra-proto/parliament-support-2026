We are currently operating within our established service standard and providing a resolution or update within fifteen (15) business days.

To help us serve you and your constituents efficiently, please ensure that referrals meet the established Problem Resolution criteria.

For more information on Canada Revenue Agency’s (CRA) Problem Resolution service, please visit Additional information.

Contact the CRA

For the telephone numbers that must be used to contact the CRA and find out the telephone wait times, please visit Contact the Canada Revenue Agency. Additionally, you will find essential information on how to resolve common questions quickly and conveniently using secure digital services on the ‘Skip the Line – Get faster help from the CRA’ page.
